  • All values in milliseconds. Vowels are decided by time only: shorter than 250 ms types the letter, longer gives the accent; they type on release.
  • Sticky keys apply to the next key only and expire after 1.5 s. Tapping Ctrl then Shift stacks them. They can also be held normally.
  • Space / NUM has no prior-idle setting; otherwise typing port 8080 quickly would give letters instead of digits.

Design

  1. Thumbs hold, nails tap one key at a time. No pinkies, no ring-finger chords; ring fingers only reach Esc and Backspace.
  2. Long-press vowels for Italian accents, like a phone keyboard.
  3. Sticky Shift, Ctrl and Alt: tap, then the next key.
  4. ' and " are always plain, despite us intl dead keys.
  5. Super under the left thumb, a second Shift under the right. Backspace stays top-right.

Requires us intl

Accents and plain quotes are typed as us intl dead-key sequences: ` + vowel for è, ' + e for é, ' + Space for '. On a device with a plain US layout, accents come out as `e and quotes get an extra space.

Dropped from stock

Mouse keys (trackpad available), Win+V, Print Screen (mango uses Super+Shift+S), Insert, Pause, Scroll Lock, right Alt, arrow cluster (↑ kept bottom right, the rest on NAV).
